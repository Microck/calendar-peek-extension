# Privacy

Calendar Peek does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The extension does not use analytics, advertising, remote scripts, external servers, or the Google Calendar API. When you click the Calendar Peek icon for a coworker, the coworker's email address and optional display name are stored in `chrome.storage.local` only long enough to hand the request to Google Calendar. Each request expires after two minutes and is removed after use.

All calendar visibility is enforced by Google Calendar and your Google Workspace administrator. Calendar Peek cannot bypass sharing permissions or reveal information that Google Calendar does not already permit your account to see.
